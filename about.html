About Attuned

Attuned creates immersive experiences in music, sound and stillness, designed to help you come back to yourself and find a moment of peace and connection in a busy life.

The experiences

From Candlelight and Stillness Sessions to soundbath and yoga, every experience is shaped by beautiful spaces, exceptional musicians and live sound.

From candlelit rooms to historic spaces, the music, setting, light and atmosphere are considered from beginning to end.
